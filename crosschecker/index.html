It measures the slip.

On your own problems it can't read your mind, so it tells you exactly what changed between two lines.

  • 2(x + 3) − 4 → 2x + 3 − 4"Line 2 is off by −3 from the line before it, so a term was added, dropped or moved wrongly."
  • 5x + 3 = 18 → 5x = 21"Line 1 gives x = 3, but line 2 gives x = 4.2."
  • (6x + 4)/2 → 6x + 4"Line 2 is 2 times the line before it, so a multiply or divide went wrong."

It knows where your class is.

Tick the topics your course has covered. Anything you haven't reached yet gets flagged before you start.

It won't do your homework.

For the night before the quiz, when office hours are closed. The worked solution is the last thing it shows, never the first.

What it checks, and what it can't yet

Checks today

  • Arithmetic, line by line (rounding to about 0.5% is allowed)
  • Algebra steps: expanding, simplifying, collecting terms
  • Solving equations, where every line must keep the same solution
  • Six practice problems in stats, micro and calculus, with hand-written hints

Can't yet

  • Read an uploaded photo or PDF on its own. You type the lines in.
  • Solve a brand-new problem for you
  • Check word answers, graphs or proofs
  • Read your real course materials. The courses here are demo data.
