Census definition: “establishments primarily engaged in furnishing academic courses and granting degrees at baccalaureate or graduate levels.” Community colleges (611210) are a separate industry.

Draft: Ivy to write

Project summary, 150–250 words. The handout asks for: the industry and why it matters to me, what the data shows, how generative AI is changing teaching and assessment, and where I fit.

From my interview: as a math UGTA I have watched courses shift toward understanding concepts, questions AI can’t always solve, and proctored pen-and-paper exams.

Research questions (first draft, Oct 15)

  • How are U.S. colleges changing assessment (exams, assignments, AI-use policies) in response to generative AI?
  • How widely do students and faculty use generative AI, and for what?
  • Is the industry growing, stable or shrinking, and what life-cycle stage is it in?
  • How might generative AI change roles: instructors, TAs, tutors, instructional technology staff?
  • Does the way a course handles AI affect how students see the course and how they perform?
